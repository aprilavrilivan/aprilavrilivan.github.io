Final Project Featured

Calibrated Reward Ensembles for LLM RLHF

I studied offline preference optimization, reward modeling, and online RL for Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, then developed a calibrated three-model reward ensemble for more reliable candidate reranking.

0.8609 average win rate for the selected offline reranking method across three course-evaluator runs.

Homework 1

Imitation Learning

Action chunking with MSE loss and flow matching on Push-T, followed by robustness experiments under uneven friction.

Flow matching reached approximately 0.802 evaluation reward at 70k steps, compared with approximately 0.576 for MSE.

Policy Gradients

REINFORCE with reward-to-go, neural baselines, and generalized advantage estimation across classic control tasks.

Q-Learning & Actor-Critic

DQN and Double DQN for discrete control, plus Soft Actor-Critic and automatic temperature tuning for continuous control.

Clipped double-Q reached roughly 3,344 return on Hopper, versus roughly 984 with a single-Q backup.

Homework 4

Reinforcement Learning for LLMs

REINFORCE and GRPO training for format following and mathematical reasoning with Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B.

GRPO reached 37.70% boxed exact match on Math Hard, compared with 28.32% for REINFORCE.

Offline Reinforcement Learning
