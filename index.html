I’m Yifan Xu, and I also go by Ivan. My research interests lie in learning-based robot control and perception, as well as model-based robot control.

I’m currently an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Engineering at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. During my junior year, I was a visiting student at the University of California, Berkeley through the GLOBE program, where I studied Computer Science. I’m currently conducting summer research with the Existential Robotics Laboratory at the University of California, San Diego. I was also fortunate to participate in the Yale Summer Session in June 2024. Before university, I graduated from Shenzhen Middle School.
